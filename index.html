Our Mission & Vision

Agape Boracay Academy Inc. is a non-stock, non-profit institution committed to developing and raising up future leaders. Guided by our faith and inspired by Psalm 119:105 — "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path" — we provide holistic education that nurtures the mind, body, and spirit.

As the School of Zion, we are proud to be recognized by the Department of Education (DepEd), ensuring our students receive quality, standards-based education right here in the beautiful island of Boracay.
